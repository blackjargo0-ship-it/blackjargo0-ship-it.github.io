About

Kurd Tech POS is a cashier program made by Kurd Tech, Kurdistan Region, Iraq. Contact: blackjargo0@gmail.com, +964 750 397 6979.

Your data

Products, sales, customers and settings are saved on the store's own computer or phone. We do not receive or sell this data.

Google Drive

  • Signing in with Google is optional. The app asks for the drive.file permission and your email.
  • The app can only see files it created itself (the backups in "Kurd Tech Backups").
  • Backups go straight from your device to your own Drive.
  • You can disconnect any time in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Use of data from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Branches

If branches are used, product names and prices, branch names and branch orders are kept on our server so the branches can see them. Costs, stock and customer data are not sent.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data and do not use it for ads.

Deleting data

Delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Delete Drive backups in Google Drive. For branch data on our server, email us.
