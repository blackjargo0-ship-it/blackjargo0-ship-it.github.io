Privacy Policy

Kurd Tech POS · last updated 24 October 2026

Who we are

Kurd Tech POS ("the app") is a cashier and store management program made by Kurd Tech, Kurdistan Region of Iraq. Contact: blackjargo0@gmail.com, +964 750 397 6979.

Data stored on your device

Products, prices, sales, customers, suppliers, users and settings are stored on the store's own computer or phone. Kurd Tech does not receive or sell this data.

Google Drive backups (optional)

  • If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks for the drive.file permission and your email address.
  • With drive.file the app can only see and change files that the app itself created — your backup files in the "Kurd Tech Backups" folder. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Backup files are sent directly from your device to your Google Drive. They are not sent to Kurd Tech.
  • The sign-in token is kept only on your device. You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings → Backup) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Branches (optional)

If the store owner turns on branches, the product names, prices and units, the branch names, and the orders that branches send are kept on Kurd Tech's online server (hosted by Supabase) so the branches and the main store can see them. Costs, stock and customer data are not sent. Branch passwords are stored only as secure hashes. The owner can turn branches off and delete branch accounts in the app.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Data on your device is deleted when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Backups in your Google Drive are deleted by deleting them in Drive. To delete branch data from the server, contact us at the email above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
