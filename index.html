Kurd Tech POS

Kurd Tech is a point-of-sale and store management program for Windows and Android: barcode sales, stock, customers and debts, suppliers, reports, receipt printing, branches, and backups to the store owner's own Google Drive.

The Google Drive connection is optional. When the store owner signs in with Google, the app only creates and reads its own backup files in a folder named "Kurd Tech Backups" in that owner's Drive.
